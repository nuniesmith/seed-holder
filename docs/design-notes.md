## Interpretation used in v0.1

| Decision | v0.1 choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Inside or outside dimensions | Inside | The bag needs the full space; the outside grows by 4.8 mm |
| "L" | Height | The sketch draws 8" vertically on the front face |
| Hook throat | 31.75 mm clear gap | Room for a bucket wall plus the rolled rim lip |
| Hook leg | 101.6 mm from the top outer surface | Matches the side-view label |
| Number of hooks | Two, 25 mm wide, 110 mm apart | Stops the pouch rotating on the rim; uses less plastic than a full-width hook |
| Top | Open, no lid | The sketch shows none |

## Bag fit

A quart-size freezer bag is about 7 × 8 in (178 × 203 mm) when flat. Once it holds
seeds it is narrower than that, so a 6.75 in inside width should be fine. The 8 in
height puts the bag's zipper about level with the pouch top. Confirm with the actual
bag brand and size you use.

## Print orientation

The pouch is taller (205.6 mm) than it is wide, so it prints upright with the open top up.
That keeps the walls as clean perimeters, needs no bridge across the floor, and fits the
Ender 3 Neo's 250 mm Z. The trade-off is that the hook legs start in mid-air and need
support. Lying the pouch on its back would put the hooks into the bed, and lying it on
its front would make the back wall a 176 mm bridge.
